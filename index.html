Major Environmental Issues

🏭

Air Pollution

Vehicles, factories and burning of waste release harmful gases that affect our health and climate.

💧

Water Scarcity

Over-use and pollution of rivers and groundwater is making clean drinking water harder to find.

🌳

Deforestation

Cutting forests destroys wildlife habitats and reduces the Earth's ability to absorb carbon dioxide.

🛍️

Plastic Waste

Single-use plastic takes hundreds of years to break down and ends up in soil, rivers and oceans.

Daily Eco Tips

💡

Switch to LED

LED bulbs use up to 80% less electricity than regular bulbs.

🔌

Unplug Devices

Chargers and TVs consume power even when switched off. Unplug them.

🚿

Shorter Showers

Reducing your shower by 2 minutes can save many litres of water daily.

🌧️

Harvest Rainwater

Collect rainwater for gardening and cleaning purposes.

♻️

Reduce, Reuse, Recycle

Carry a cloth bag and a steel bottle instead of using plastic.

🍃

Compost Kitchen Waste

Turn vegetable peels into natural manure for plants.
